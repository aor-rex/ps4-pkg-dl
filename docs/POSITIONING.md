# POSITIONING.md — ps4-pkg-dl landing page

## Brand Script (StoryBrand)

- **Character:** A Linux user with a personal PS4 collection who wants it
  organized, visual, and downloading without babysitting.
- **Problem:**
  - External: PKGs scattered across folders, cryptic filenames, downloads
    that die at 80%.
  - Internal: babysitting transfers feels like a chore; re-downloading
    large files stings.
  - Philosophical: managing your own files should not take a sysadmin
    project.
  - Villain: scattered scripts, dead forum threads, downloaders that
    cannot resume.
- **Guide:** Empathy — built by someone tired of re-downloading dead
  transfers. Authority — MIT open-source: every line inspectable, no
  account, no telemetry. (No user counts or testimonials exist yet and
  none are claimed.)
- **Plan (process):** 1. Paste one command. 2. Point the app at your
  catalog. 3. Pick a game and download.
- **Plan (agreement):** Free. No account. Your files stay yours.
- **CTA:** Direct — the installer command. Transitional — View on GitHub.
- **Failure:** Another evening watching a dead transfer; the collection
  stays a folder of cryptic filenames.
- **Success:** Open the app, see your library with covers, downloads
  finish while you do something else.

## One-Liner

"Stop babysitting your PS4 downloads — point the app at your catalog and
let it finish the job."

## Key Messages

- Outcome-first: "Resume dead transfers", not "Download manager".
- Guide voice: empathy first, credentials brief (open code, MIT).
- No proof claims beyond what exists: version number, license, platform,
  test suite size. No users, no download counts, no testimonials.
